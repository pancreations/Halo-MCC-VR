Strict reading
--------------
+0x1012E2 and +0x101300 are both zero in the measured idle window and nonzero
in both firing windows, with equal deltas inside each window. That is enough
to justify a one-event, pausing observation at +0x1012E2.

The counts are aggregate site reaches: no particle-system identity predicate
was applied. They do not by themselves distinguish AR particle A/B/C or prove
a position/matrix difference.

The zero counter at +0x100EBC is unresolved instrumentation evidence, not
proof that the helper was skipped. The pinned retail call instruction at
+0x1012E2 directly targets +0x100EBC; Phase 2B step-over evidence will test
that relationship directly.

The high +0x1059B8 volume is shared resolver traffic and is not attributed to
the visible muzzle effect from these counts alone.
